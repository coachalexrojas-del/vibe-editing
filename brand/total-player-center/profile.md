# Total Player Center (TPC) — brand profile
Source: owner's interview + "TPC Brand & Operating Philosophy" questionnaire (Rev1, Nov 2026).
Questionnaire header name: "TPC Baseball & Softball". Logo source: thepitchingcenter.com.

## Identity
- Name: Total Player Center / TPC (owner's 9-5 brand)
- Logo: `logos/tpc-logo.png` (800x263, transparent PNG: gray "TPC" + blue/green chevrons)
- Voice: Direct, knowledgeable, practical, demanding with a purpose. Explain the "why" in plain
  language. Encourage athletes while holding them accountable. Show real coaching and honest
  progress. Speak to both baseball AND softball families.
- AVOID: hype, promises of guaranteed results, making every story about a number (e.g. peak velo).
- Colors:
  - Gray (lettering/ring/stitching) #8B8E95
  - Blue (upper chevron) #0191D4
  - Bright blue (lower chevron) #01BDF9
  - Dark green (upper chevron) #90A81E
  - Lime green (lower chevron) #BAD629
  - White (background/lettering) #FFFFFF
- Font style: bold, clean sans-serif headings; simple, readable body text.

## Audience
Baseball/softball athletes (youth → college → pro) and their parents. Pain: conflicting advice,
disconnected lessons, chasing isolated numbers, pressure for quick results. Parents want a plan
they understand. Main platform: Instagram (short teaching clips), then YouTube.

## Content pillars (what makes a clip worth cutting)
1. HOW DEVELOPMENT WORKS — skill, strength, mental prep, arm care, recovery and PT working together
   in one plan; program phases; guided discovery.
2. PROGRESS WITH CONTEXT — progress in movement, execution, operating velocity, training capacity,
   mental routines, readiness; how feedback informs the next step.
3. THE TAP IN STANDARD — Attitude, Effort, Approach; active coaching, athlete effort, staff
   education, facility care, community.

## Brand commandments
1. Tap In: bring Attitude, Effort, and Approach to every session.
2. Coach the athlete: listen, meet each athlete where they are at, teach.
3. Earn trust: show real progress and deliver the TPC Standard daily.
